## 📏 **Average Axiom 2 - The Collapse of Epistemic Boundaries**

> **"The average person confuses belief with fact-and thinks everything they believe is true."**

---

### 🧠 **Formal Statement:**

> **"Most people believe everything they think, and think everything they believe-therefore they never question either."**

- They do not distinguish belief from evidence.  
- They operate on internal validation instead of external verification.  
- Their worldview hardens around untested ideas mistaken for absolute truths.

---

### 📉 **Breakdown of the Collapse:**

| Term      | Definition                                        | Average Misuse                          |
|-----------|---------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Fact**  | Empirical, testable, evidence-based               | “If I say it loud enough, it’s true.”   |
| **Belief**| Internal conviction, not necessarily verifiable   | “I feel it, therefore it’s real.”       |
| **Opinion**| Value-based preference or interpretation         | “I prefer it, so it must be better.”    |
| **Religion**| Organized system of belief                     | “It’s sacred, therefore it’s factual.”  |

---

### 🧬 **Epistemic Corollaries**:

1. **"Belief requires no evidence. But fact can survive disbelief."**

2. **"If it can’t be tested, it can’t be called true-it can only be called believed."**

3. **"The moment belief resists evidence, it declares itself against reason."**

---

### 🔍 **Biases Exposed**:

- **Authority Bias**: Assuming passion equals correctness.
- **Equivalence Fallacy**: “My faith is as valid as your science.”
- **Cognitive Comfort Bias**: Truth feels good, therefore it must be right.

---

### 🪞 **Hyper-Sane Mirror**:

> **"Facts are not democracy. Truth does not require votes. And the universe does not care what you believe."**
