[Thirsty ChatGPT GPT3](https://www.mepmiddleeast.com/news/thirsty-chatgpt-microsoft-openais-gpt-3-training-consumes-185000-gallons-of-water) - April 19, 2023 11:16 AM GST



The researchers investigated the water usage in data processing centres used by companies like OpenAI and Google. They found that Microsoft, in partnership with OpenAI, consumed a staggering 185,000 gallons of water just for training GPT-3, which is equivalent to the amount of water needed to cool a nuclear reactor. The paper further notes that the water used by Microsoft to cool its data centres in the United States for training GPT-3 could have been used to produce “370 BMW cars or 320 Tesla electric vehicles.” If the model had been trained in Microsoft’s larger data centres in Asia, the water consumption would have tripled.
