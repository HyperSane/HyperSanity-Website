##  Water Usage in AI Model Training

The training of large AI models also consumes water, primarily for cooling data centers. For instance, training OpenAI's GPT-3 model consumed approximately 700,000 liters (about 185,000 gallons) of water.

In contrast, a single ChatGPT query uses about 0.000085 gallons of water, equivalent to roughly one-fifteenth of a teaspoon.

---

##  Comparative Analysis

To put these figures into perspective:

- Producing one pound of beef requires approximately 1,800 gallons of water.
    
- Training GPT-3 consumed about 185,000 gallons of water.
    

This means that the water used to train GPT-3 is equivalent to the water needed to produce about 103 pounds of beef.
