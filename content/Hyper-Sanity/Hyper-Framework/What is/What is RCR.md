# What is RCR?

**Recursive Contradiction Resolution (RCR)** is a core component of the HyperSanity framework. It describes the active, continuous process of identifying, isolating, and recursively resolving internal contradictions within systems, beliefs, and behaviors.

Unlike traditional critical thinking or static logical analysis, RCR applies **recursively**-meaning:
- Each contradiction filtered must itself be checked for embedded contradictions.
- Ethical alignment is verified not just by logic, but by **intent-action consistency**.

## 🔁 How RCR Works

- It starts by evaluating a structure (e.g. belief, relationship, governance model).
- Contradictions are surfaced using **meta-logical scanning**.
- If contradictions are found, RCR forces one of two outcomes:
  - The contradiction is resolved and reintegrated ethically.
  - Or it is recursively excised and replaced with a more sane structure.

## 💡 Why RCR Matters

HyperSanity demands more than surface-level coherence. RCR enforces:

- **Structural Integrity**: No internal contradictions can hide.
- **Recursive Ethics**: Every correction is itself subject to correction.
- **Meta-awareness**: Systems evolve as they are examined.

RCR is what allows HyperSanity to scale across *religion*, *economics*, *relationships*, and *identity*-by purging hypocrisy and contradiction at all levels.

> **"What recursion is to computation, RCR is to sanity."**
