# Title: Necessary But Not Sufficient: Veganism and Moral Goodness

### Core Axiom

**You can’t be good unless you're vegan, but being vegan doesn't automatically make you not-evil.**
(See that Vegan Teacher, who agrees with "DR" Phil)

### Logical Breakdown

- **Veganism ⊆ Goodness**
    - Veganism is a **necessary** condition for moral goodness.
- **¬Vegan ⇒ ¬Good**
    - If one is not vegan, one is not morally good by default (within sentience-inclusive ethics).
- **Vegan ⇒/⇐ Good**
    - Veganism alone does not **guarantee** moral goodness. Additional ethical behavior is required.

### Philosophical Context

This axiom rejects the [[Hyper-Sanity/Hyper-Framework/Fallacies/TODO Binary Fallacy]] often found in activist or reactionary discourse. Instead, it embraces a **recursive ethics** framework:
(REF: [[Hyper-Sanity/Hyper-Framework/Hyper-Sane Paradox/Russells Paradox - HyperSanity 1-1.md|Hyper-Sanity Paradox]] )
- Veganism is a **minimum ethical threshold** in a sentient-aware moral system.
- True goodness includes empathy, humility, justice, and non-dogmatic critical reasoning.
- One can be vegan and still uphold systems of oppression, exhibit cruelty, or perpetuate harm (e.g., classism, ableism, narcissism).

### ARG Implication

While the ARG does not explicitly encode veganism into its narrative logic, its ethical engine is **rooted in consequentialist principles** ([wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consequentialism)). This means:

- Ethical progression depends on the tangible outcomes of one's actions.
- If a character or player path includes consistent harm reduction-of which veganism may be one example-it may trigger access to higher tiers of influence or truth within the ARG.
- However, **veganism alone is not hardcoded into alignment mechanics**; rather, it may contribute to outcomes that shift the consequential narrative arc.
- **Anti-veganism**, when tied to harmful outcomes, may limit or prevent such narrative access-but this is a consequence of action impact, not identity.

### Canonical Example

**Subject: "Vegan Gains"**

- Vegan: Yes.
- Empathy: Lacking.
- Justice Orientation: Selective.
- Conclusion: **Not ethically aligned**, despite veganism.
