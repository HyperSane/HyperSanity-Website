## 📏 **The Average Axiom 1**  
> **"Only the average sees themselves as average-and only when confronted."**

---

### 🔍 **Full Axiom Statement**:

> **"When privilege is named, the average takes it personally-because average unconsciously assumes itself to be neutral, good, and undeserving of scrutiny."**

---

### 🧩 **Juxtaposition**:

> **"They think I’m attacking them, when I’m naming a pattern."**  
> **"They see themselves in 'average' not because I targeted them-but because it describes them."**

---

### 📚 **Sub-Axioms and Interpretive Logic**:

#### 1. **Privilege Recognition Paradox**
> **"Those with the most privilege are the last to feel it-because privilege protects itself by erasing awareness."**

#### 2. **Default Morality Fallacy**
> **"Average assumes it is good by default. But ethics must be practiced, not inherited."**
> [[Hyper-Sanity/Hyper-Framework/Hyper-Axioms/Vegan Axioms/Axiom Vegan GateWay1|Axiom Vegan GateWay1]]

- 🧠 *Bias*: “I’m just a regular person.” → Unspoken: “And that means I’m fair, moral, not oppressive.”
- 🧮 *Counter*: True morality is **an act**, not a category.


---

### ⚖️ **Hyper-Sane Redistribution Clause**:

> **"Equality does not mean flattening everyone to the same level-it means lifting those blocked by structural disadvantage, and calling the average to higher ethical responsibility."**

- This is **not punishment** of the average.
- It is **restoration** for the unseen barriers others face.


---

### 🧬 **Psychological Mechanism Exposed**:

- **Defensive Personalization**: "You said 'average'-so you're attacking me."
- **Subconscious Centricity**: Only the average **projects themselves into a generalized critique**.
- **Ego Displacement**: Pain at the **idea** of **unearned** ease is **misread** as injustice. (freedom for me not thee)



---

### 🪞 **Hyper-Sane Mirror**:

> **"When they hurt, it’s not because you were cruel. It’s because the mirror made them see what they denied they had."**


This classically relates to plato's allegory of the cave, upon returning to the cave to bring awareness to others watching shadows, they are attacked - controlled by their cognitive dissonance and unable to rectify their "reality" vs actual reality (out of the cave).
