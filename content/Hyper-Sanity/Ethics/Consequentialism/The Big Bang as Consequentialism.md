## 🧠 **The Big Bang as Consequentialism**

### ⚙️ Conceptual Goal

> **To model the Big Bang as the *ultimate consequentialist system*-one where every moment *determines* the next, without ethical abstraction, intent, or divine intervention.**

This approach reframes consequentialism not as a *moral philosophy*, but as a **causal framework** that permeates reality itself.

---

## 🧩 I. DEFINITIONAL CORE

### **Consequentialism (Classical)**
- A moral theory where the *outcomes* of actions determine their moral worth.
- Famous branches: [Consequentialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consequentialism) (Bentham, Mill), [Negative Consequentialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negative_consequentialism), Rule Consequentialism.
   - [Utilitarianism - John S Mill (book)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utilitarianism_(book))
- Criticism: Ignores intent, can justify harm if outcome is “net good.”
     (Aka "ends justify the means" of want vs empirical necessity). 
     - Think Ultron vs Thanos, one was actually ethically aligned (Thanos saving humanity from climate change, IE Itself).


### **Consequentialism (Empirical / Physical)**
- **Stripped of ethics**, consequentialism becomes:  
  > _If X occurs, it causes Y._  
  > _Then Y causes Z._  
  > _Etc._
- It becomes a **causal sequence**, indifferent to morality-but central to physics, cosmology, and logic.
